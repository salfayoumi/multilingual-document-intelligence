# Rotating Equipment Monitoring Guide

## Vibration escalation

The vibration warning threshold is 7.1 mm/s RMS. An operator should escalate the warning to the maintenance lead when the measurement remains above that threshold for at least 10 consecutive seconds. A single short spike should be recorded, but it does not require escalation.

The emergency shutdown threshold is 11.2 mm/s RMS. If that level is reached, stop the motor through the normal safety circuit and follow the lockout procedure before inspection.

## Temperature response

Normal bearing temperature is 45–72 °C under steady load. At 82 °C, reduce the load and inspect lubrication flow. At 90 °C, stop the equipment and investigate before restarting.

## Evidence retention

Store the preceding five minutes of sensor readings with the incident record. Include vibration, bearing temperature, motor current, load, and rotational speed.
