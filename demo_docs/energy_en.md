# Facility Energy Review

## Compressed-air losses

The monthly energy review treats a compressed-air leak as high priority when its estimated annual loss exceeds 4,000 kWh. Repairs should be verified with a second ultrasonic measurement within seven days.

## Idle equipment

Equipment that draws more than 2 kW while idle for longer than four hours per day should be included in the automatic shutdown assessment. Safety, warm-up time, and production constraints must be reviewed before automation is enabled.
